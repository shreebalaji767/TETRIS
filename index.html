HOW TO PLAY

Move falling blocks to complete a solid horizontal row. Completed rows disappear and earn points. Keep the stack low — the game ends if blocks reach the top.

KEYBOARD

TOUCH

Use the on-screen buttons. You can also tap the board to rotate, swipe sideways to move, swipe down to soft-drop, or swipe up to hard-drop.

PRO TIP Clear four rows at once for a Tetris! Use HOLD to save a piece for later and watch the ghost piece to see where your block will land.
